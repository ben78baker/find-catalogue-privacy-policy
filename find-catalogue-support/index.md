# Find Catalogue support

Find Catalogue is a private, local-first catalogue for field finds on iPhone, iPad, Android, and macOS.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email [bmbapps@icloud.com](mailto:bmbapps@icloud.com).

Please include:

- the device and operating-system version;
- the Find Catalogue app version;
- what you were trying to do; and
- the wording of any error message.

Do not email private coordinates, photographs, or a catalogue export unless they are necessary and you have deliberately chosen to share them.

We aim to reply within 48 hours.

## Common checks

### Camera, photographs, or location are unavailable

Review Find Catalogue's permissions in the device Settings app. A manual record can use a photograph from the library and can have a manually entered, approximate, or unknown discovery location.

### A map is blank or unavailable

Interactive maps need an internet connection to load OpenStreetMap tiles. Your catalogue and coordinates remain stored locally and can still be used without the map.

### Sharing a PDF-and-photos bundle

The bundle is a ZIP file containing a formatted PDF, a README manifest, and saveable photographs grouped by permanent log number. Choose whether exact coordinates are hidden or included before creating the export.

### Records or photographs appear to be missing

Do not delete or reinstall the app while investigating, because the catalogue is stored in the app's local container. Contact support with the device, app version, and the steps that led to the issue.
